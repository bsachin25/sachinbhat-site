Services

SEO & AEO services

I help businesses get found on Google and in AI answers. These are the services I offer. You can take one on its own or combine a few, based on what your website needs.

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)

Ongoing SEO for your website, covering keyword research, on-page optimisation, content planning and internal linking, so your pages rank for what your customers search for.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation)

Structuring your content so AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI Overviews can understand it, quote it and mention your brand in their answers.

Content Writing

SEO content for every part of your website: webpage copy, blogs, product pages, category pages and any other content that needs to rank.

Technical Audit

A detailed check of your site's technical health, including internal links, schema, sitemaps, Core Web Vitals, keyword cannibalisation and more. You get a clear list of fixes to work through with your developer.

Complete Website Audit

A full review of your website from an SEO point of view. It covers everything in the technical audit, plus a content audit of your pages and blogs, so you know what to fix and improve first.

International SEO

SEO for businesses that sell in more than one country, such as India, the USA, the UK and the UAE, with correct hreflang setup and content planned for each market's search habits.

SEO Strategy & Consultation

For businesses with their own team. I prepare a 3-month SEO strategy based on your team's capacity, and you can consult me every week while your team carries it out. I am not involved in the execution.

Page layout designs

With any of these services, I can share page designs for blogs, landing pages, service pages and product pages. These designs show the layout and structure I recommend, so you can see how the page should come together. They are not final designs. For the finished version in your brand guidelines, you will need a designer.

Let's talk about your website.

Whether you are launching a new website or improving an existing one, send me your website and a line about your goals. I will share where I think SEO can help, and what to focus on first.
