eCommerce SEO questions, answered

Straight answers to what store owners search for most.

How do I improve SEO for my eCommerce website?

Start with your money pages. Give every product and category page an original description, a clear title, product schema and strong internal links, since these are the pages closest to a sale. Then build blog content around what buyers search before they purchase, and fix technical basics like indexing and site speed so Google can crawl your full catalog.

Why are my product pages not ranking on Google?

Most product pages fail to rank because they use the manufacturer description that hundreds of other stores also use. Google sees nothing original to reward. Rewrite them in your own words, add real customer reviews, link them from related pages and category pages, and add product schema so Google can read price, stock and ratings.

How do I get my brand to show up in ChatGPT and AI answers?

To appear in ChatGPT, Claude and other AI answers, publish clear, answer-first content that states the answer in the first line, backed by original information and proper schema markup. AI tools quote sources they can understand and trust, so unique data, genuine reviews and real expertise matter far more than keyword stuffing.

How can my store appear in Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews pull from pages that answer a question directly and clearly. Structure your content with a plain question as the heading and a direct answer right below it, keep the information factual and original, and use FAQ and product schema. Pages that already rank well and read cleanly are the ones most often pulled into the overview.

Do I need an SEO consultant for my online store?

If your store has traffic but few sales, product pages that do not rank, or a catalog too large to manage alone, an SEO consultant is worth it. A consultant plans around your buyers and your margins, fixes the technical and content gaps holding you back, and reports on sales and leads rather than vanity metrics.

How do I increase organic sales for my online store?

Organic sales grow when you rank the pages people buy from, not just blog pages that bring readers. Target buyer-intent keywords, make your category and product pages clear and trustworthy, add comparison and buying-guide content for people deciding between options, and remove friction in the path from search result to checkout.
