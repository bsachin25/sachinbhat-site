A look at the impact of my SEO work, shown with actual data. Here are two stores I have helped grow over the last six months, with their results straight from Google Search Console.

Jiya Gold Palace

Heritage jewellery brand • jiyagoldpalace.com

0 to 1 SEO launch • 6 months

Jiya Gold Palace had run as an offline brand since 1987, with almost no presence in search. I led the SEO for the online store from scratch and single-handedly: building out product pages, writing category and blog content, shaping the page design, and coordinating with the developers to keep the site healthy. The store also started appearing inside Google's AI features, reaching 38.6K impressions there. This is the AEO side of the work paying off, getting the brand in front of people using AI-powered search.

  • Technical SEO and coordination with developers

The Desi Food

Indian food & grocery for NRIs • thedesifood.com

Full revamp • 25,000+ products • 6 months

The Desi Food sells Indian food and groceries to NRIs around the world, with no deliveries inside India, so the whole audience is international. I was involved in a complete website revamp covering 25,000+ product pages, 200+ category pages and 150+ blogs. After the revamp, I set up AI-assisted workflows for product page optimization, category page optimization, blog writing and internal linking. Since then, impressions have grown steadily in the USA and other international markets, even without hreflang in place. The charts below are from Google Search Console, filtered to the USA.
