Services

eCommerce SEO & AEO services

I help eCommerce brands get found on Google and in AI answers. Below are the services I offer, from product description writing and SEO blog content to technical SEO and AI search visibility. Most clients start with one or two of these, based on what their store needs first.

eCommerce SEO

End-to-end SEO for online stores, covering product pages, category pages and the overall site structure, so your store ranks for the terms that bring buyers.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Structuring your content so AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI Overviews can understand it, quote it and mention your brand in their answers.

Product Description Writing

Original, SEO-friendly product descriptions that help pages rank and give shoppers the details they need to buy.

Category Page Optimization

Making your category and collection pages rank with clear copy, the right keywords, clean structure and internal links, since these are often your highest-value pages.

SEO Blog Writing

Blog content planned around buyer intent and built into topic clusters, so your store attracts the right readers and builds authority in your niche.

Keyword Research & Strategy

Finding the keywords your buyers actually use and turning them into a clear content and page plan, so effort goes where it brings sales.

Technical SEO

Site audits and fixes for indexing, crawl issues, site speed, Core Web Vitals, sitemaps and schema, worked through together with your developer.

Topical Authority & Content Clusters

Building topic maps and well-linked content clusters that help search engines and AI tools see your brand as an expert in your category.

On-Page & Design Suggestions for SEO

Practical suggestions on page layout, structure, headings and internal linking that make a page easier to rank and use. This is guidance for your designer or developer, not a website design service.

International SEO

Geo-targeted SEO for stores selling across countries like India, the USA, the UK and the UAE, with correct hreflang setup and content planned for each market's search habits.
